MEDMORY

autocadconcept2025

As we age, our consumption of medicine increases while our memory begins to fog. This means many elderly forget if they’ve taken their daily doses, leading to dangerous instances of overdose, especially for those with dementia and Alzheimer’s.

Medmory’s smart technology makes it more than just a medicine box: according to the user’s dosage times, Medmory will rotate itself to expose a brand new set of pills ready for consumption, providing health and safety to those who need it most.
